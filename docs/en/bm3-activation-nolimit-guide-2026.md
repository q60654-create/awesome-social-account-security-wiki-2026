> **Key Takeaway:** How long a BM3 lasts depends on whether four tracks are aligned before activation, whether the three ad accounts start in sequence, and whether first-week spend is logged and reversible. Activating a sick asset saves hours you will repay with the whole business manager.

BM3 means a Business Manager with an allowance of three ad accounts, the most common asset container for small media-buying teams and studios. In troubleshooting I see the same failure repeatedly: accounts do not die during the campaign phase, they die from bad records left during activation. This article follows the order I work tickets, from the pre-activation checklist to activation sequence, first-week pacing, and what to do when the allowance drops. Every step gives an executable action.

## Why can't you skip the pre-activation checklist?

Do not put an unprepared BM3 online. Hours saved by activating a sick asset are usually repaid with the entire business manager. Confirm each item below; if any one fails, fix it before activation.

- The personal account carrying the BM is healthy: no pending verification reminders, no security warnings, no recent abnormal login records;

- Two-Step Verification is on and recovery codes are backed up and archived;

- Environment is ready: a fixed browser profile plus a fixed residential IP exit, and this environment has never been linked to restricted assets;

- Business materials are complete: company name, address, a reachable website domain consistent with the business, and at least one fully populated Page;

- Payment is prepared: the card is confirmed usable, the billing address is logically consistent with the IP location, and a small test budget is reserved.

The point of this checklist is to align four tracks in advance: personal account, environment, business materials, and payment. If any track is flawed, reviews and charges during activation will expose the problem, and the later it surfaces, the higher the repair cost. Most "restricted on activation" tickets I handle trace back to one of these four tracks being out of alignment.

## How do you activate a BM3 step by step?

Activation itself feeds the system a signal that this is a normal merchant; the more rushed the actions, the worse the signal. Advance in the order below and leave an interval between steps.

1. Create the BM in the fixed environment with the healthy personal account, and fill in business information truthfully;

2. After email confirmation, complete settings: verify the domain, link the Page, and invite only necessary members;

3. Create the first ad account and **set time zone and currency correctly the first time**; do not change them later;

4. Bind the payment method and wait for the bank pre-authorization charge to confirm;

5. Run one routine creative ad at the lowest budget and walk the full review, delivery, and charge chain;

6. Only after the first account shows normal data, activate the second and third accounts — **never start all three on the same day**.

The value of step 6 is this: the core advantage of BM3 is spreading risk across three accounts, but starting all three at once makes the risk happen simultaneously. Let the first account complete the full chain, then copy the experience to the second and third; that is spreading, not stacking. I recommend at least a 24-hour interval here, depending on account status.

## How should you pace spend from day one to week one?

The goal of the first week is not volume; it is building a trust record. Advance at this pace:

- Day 1: settings and testing only, budget at the lowest tier, to verify the chain works rather than to spend;

- Days 2–3: keep the low budget, focus on review speed and charge stability, and **record every review result**;

- Days 4–7: raise budget in steps only if data is clean, keep each increase conservative, log everything, and pause increases immediately if fluctuation appears.

The key is "logged" and "reversible." Every budget increase has a record, so when review slows or a charge fails you can quickly locate which action caused the fluctuation instead of guessing while multiple variables change at once. For specific budget tiers, follow the live quote on the page; I do not hard-code numbers here because trust starting points differ between accounts.

## What do you do first and later when the allowance drops?

Allowance drops are not rare; the key is to find the cause before acting. Common causes include: first-week spend too aggressive for the account's current trust level; creative or landing page triggering policy review; payment abnormalities such as failed charges, declined cards, or billing disputes; environment drift after changing IP or device; and linked assets (Page, pixel, personal account) dragging the BM down.

Handle in this order:

1. **Slow down first**: no major actions for 48 hours, observe only;

2. Check the account quality panel and system notifications to find the real restriction reason before acting; guessing only makes it worse;

3. Resolve payment issues on the payment side first: confirm the charging card status matches the billing address, then consider a backup card;

4. Retire accounts confirmed unrecoverable promptly and migrate the Page and pixel to reserve accounts; do not let a dead account drag down the whole BM's trust rating.

One reminder: an allowance drop does not mean the account is scrap, but repeatedly "rescuing" it with the same actions often turns a recoverable restriction into an unrecoverable one. Stopping to observe is itself a form of handling.

## What is the difference between a BM3 with a replacement guarantee and one without?

When evaluating BM3 assets, whether a replacement guarantee applies directly changes how risk is carried. The table below compares approach, process, and risk dimensions, with no price comparison.

| Dimension | BM3 with replacement guarantee | BM3 without guarantee | Decision point |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Risk bearer | Carries part of activation-phase risk per agreement | Risk fully borne by the user | Confirm the coverage scope and criteria are written clearly |

| Best fit | Small teams just starting with limited warming experience | Teams with mature environment and warming process | Check whether the team already has stable environment and payment chain |

| Usage constraints | Usually requires compliant activation and spend; violations may void it | Fewer constraints, but issues must be self-diagnosed | Confirm whether password change, rebinding, or automation tools fall outside coverage |

| Appeal materials | Order number, first-login time, error screenshots, password-change statement | No unified intake; self-review | Incomplete materials slow down diagnosis |

| Main risk | Misjudged as a usage issue and rejected | All activation-phase issues self-borne | Both require environment isolation first |

Whichever you choose, the pre-activation checklist and first-week pacing cannot be skipped. A replacement guarantee is a risk buffer, not immunity; no guarantee does not mean problems are certain, and the key remains environment isolation and compliant spend pacing. I recommend archiving appeal materials on delivery day: order number, delivery time, first-login time with failure screenshots (including system time), the original error text, and a statement on whether the password was changed or contact rebound. Complete information is what lets a case be diagnosed as a delivery issue or a usage issue.

## Can you manage multiple BM3 assets in the same environment?

Not recommended. The checklist explicitly requires "a fixed browser profile plus a fixed residential IP exit, and this environment has never been linked to restricted assets." Sharing one environment across multiple BM3 assets creates links between them; when one fails, the others may be affected.

A safer approach is to give each BM3 its own environment and exit, and keep member invitations to the minimum necessary. Then even if one BM3 drops allowance or gets restricted, reserve assets are not affected, which matches the original design intent of spreading risk across three accounts. If you also run multiple Telegram or Discord accounts, the isolation logic is the same; see the session and device management articles in the [site blog](https://facai.lol/blog).

## Why does slower activation actually save time?

Every action during activation is recorded and amplified over the following weeks. Starting fast seems to save preparation time but concentrates risk at the most fragile stage: the personal account has no stable record yet, the payment chain is unverified, and creatives are unreviewed — any one failing affects all three accounts at once.

Conversely, completing the checklist, activating in steps, and pacing spend is slow up front, but each step accumulates reusable experience and clean records. The value of BM3 is spreading risk across three accounts. Activate a little slower, spend a little steadier, isolate a little stricter, and the asset group produces reliably over time. ## FAQ

### Can all three ad accounts in a BM3 start on the same day?

Not recommended. Let the first account complete the full review, delivery, and charge chain and confirm normal data before activating the second and third in sequence. Starting all three the same day makes risk happen simultaneously and loses the spreading benefit. The interval depends on account status; I generally suggest at least 24 hours.

### How should first-week budget be controlled to avoid allowance drops?

Day 1 is settings and testing only with the budget at the lowest tier; days 2–3 keep the low budget and observe review and charging; days 4–7 raise budget in steps only when data is clean, with conservative increases and full logging. Follow the live quote on the page for specific tiers and do not copy other people's numbers.

### What should you do first when the allowance drops?

Slow down first and only observe for 48 hours; then check the account quality panel and system notifications to find the real restriction reason before acting. Guessing only worsens the problem. For payment issues, confirm card status and billing address consistency on the payment side first.

### How should an account confirmed unrecoverable be handled?

Retire it promptly and stop spending budget trying to rescue it. Migrate the Page and pixel to reserve accounts as soon as possible to avoid dragging down the whole BM's trust rating, and review the restriction cause to prevent recurrence. If it involves a quality issue within the delivery window, prepare materials per the 24-hour first-login replacement guarantee.
