> **Key Takeaway:** Decide the account's purpose before you shop, complete the first-login test within ten minutes and keep timestamped evidence, then follow the published first-login window and guarantee boundaries if something breaks. Do not change the password first.

What decides the outcome of buying a TikTok account is not the account itself but two things: the criteria you apply **before** the purchase and the **first ten minutes** after delivery. Most disputes come from two moves — rushing to change the password and rebind contact details before confirming account state, and failing to produce timestamped screenshots when a problem appears. This guide gives executable actions in three stages: how to choose, how to test, how to claim. The service and guarantee wording follows the publicly stated rules of Facai Research (facai.lol).

## Define the use case before you choose an account

The same TikTok account serves content seeding and ad-traffic landing very differently, and the screening dimensions do not overlap. Write down three things first: what content the account will publish, how many people will operate it, and which network environment it will log in from. These three answers determine which of the following you actually need to inspect: **registration region, historical content, follower structure, and any violation history**.

Work through the checks below in order. Do not open with "how many followers does it have":

1. **Content-direction match.** Is the account's historical content in the same vertical as what you plan to publish? Taking over across verticals will scramble the recommendation feed for a while.

2. **Follower structure.** Look at the regional distribution of followers and the interaction ratio. An account whose follower count and like count are badly out of proportion will produce weak numbers after handover.

3. **Violation traces.** Check whether the account can publish normally, whether creator tools open, and whether any video shows a takedown notice.

4. **Login-environment requirements.** Confirm whether delivery is username and password only, or includes cookies or a session file. This determines how you will log in afterwards.

If you are handling accounts across several platforms at once, manage the TikTok test inside the same checklist as your Telegram and Discord delivery self-checks so nothing gets skipped. The site's guide to [2FA and login-session management](https://facai.lol/blog/2fa-login-security-session-management-2026) is useful for turning "change password, enable 2FA, clear devices" into fixed actions.

## Four hard indicators at the selection stage

The decision table below answers one question: can this account be taken over? Every row is directly observable during the test and needs no extra tooling.

| Indicator | Acceptable | Proceed with caution | Recommend abandoning |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Can publish normally | Post enters review or is immediately visible | Post succeeds but zero views persist | Publish button errors or shows a restriction notice |

| Creator tools | Opens and shows the analytics panel | Some functions greyed out | Cannot open, or a violation notice appears |

| Followers and interaction | Like and comment ratios look natural | High follower count, very low interaction | Follower list is abnormally concentrated |

| Login stability | Repeatable login from the same environment | Requires multiple verification codes | Login triggers verification that repeatedly fails |

This table makes no price judgement; it only reads account state. Running every row is far more reliable than reading a description. ## The ten-minute first-login checklist

After delivery, **test first, change the password second**. Reverse that order and you lose the ability to prove whether a problem came from delivery or from your own actions. Work through the list below, recording the screen or taking a continuous series of screenshots that include the system clock.

1. **First login.** Log in from a clean browser profile or an isolated antidetect-browser environment. Record the exact time of the first successful login — this is the starting point for any later guarantee claim.

2. **Basic information check.** Confirm the username, avatar, bio and the tail of the bound email address match the delivery description.

3. **Publish test.** Post one unimportant test item and watch whether it enters recommendation or review normally. Delete it once confirmed.

4. **Function availability.** Open creator tools, direct messages and the comment area, and confirm no function-level restriction notice appears.

5. **Login-state stability.** Log out and log in once more, confirming you are not asked for an abnormal number of verification codes.

6. **Archive the evidence.** Put screenshots, the original error text and the login time into one folder, with the date in the folder name.

Only after the test passes should you harden security: change the password, enable two-step verification, and check for unknown logged-in devices. TikTok's device management sits inside account security settings; check each device name and login location, and end any session you do not recognise. The logic matches Telegram device cleanup, and you can follow the verification method in [Telegram session and device cleanup](https://facai.lol/blog/telegram-session-device-cleanup-guide).

### The point most people miss during testing

Many buyers only check "can I log in" and never check "can I operate after logging in". An account that shows as logged in while publishing, liking and messaging all fail is usually a dead session or a server-side restriction. Stop operating immediately and preserve evidence. Do not retry repeatedly — repeated retries push platform risk controls to a higher level.

## What to do when something breaks, and where the guarantee ends

Classify the problem first, then choose a path. The directions below describe handling; whether a specific case is accepted **depends on the account state** and on the actual verification result.

1. **Cannot log in, or password changed.** Stop retrying immediately. Assemble the order number, first-login time and error screenshots, then submit through the after-sales process.

2. **Banned on delivery.** Keep a screenshot of the ban notice with the timestamp. Do not appeal on your own; submit for verification first.

3. **Functions restricted but login works.** Record which functions are restricted and the exact notice text, then judge whether the cause is account state or environment.

4. **Verification code not arriving.** First confirm the number range supports the platform, then resend through a different channel. Do not enter wrong codes repeatedly.

On guarantee scope, the public wording is: **delivery problems appearing on the first day after login are handled under the replacement guarantee; cases beyond that window, or where the password has been changed, contact details rebound, the account resold, or prohibited usage occurred, fall outside the guarantee.** The clock starts at the buyer's first successful login, not at order time. The earlier you test, the more of that window you still hold.

Prepare claim materials in four parts: order number and delivery time; first-login time plus failure screenshots including the system clock; the original error text; and a statement of whether the password was changed, contact details rebound, or any third-party tool used. Complete information is what allows a case to be classified as a delivery problem rather than a usage problem; missing items slow handling directly. The full after-sales wording is set out in the [service catalogue](https://facai.lol/services).

## How to run the account through its first seven days

Passing the test does not mean you can operate at full volume. A newly taken-over account still carries historical behaviour labels on the platform side, so keep actions modest for the first seven days:

1. Keep the login environment stable; do not switch IP or device frequently.

2. Keep daily publishing volume low and raise it gradually.

3. Do not send bulk direct messages and do not mass-follow within a short period.

4. Do not use the same login state on several devices at the same time.

If delivery is cookie-based, follow the import steps exactly: confirm a clean environment, import the complete JSON array, refresh to verify the login state. Changing the password immediately after import invalidates the session — that is expected behaviour, not a fault. The detailed steps are in [Cookie-Editor JSON import troubleshooting](https://facai.lol/blog/cookie-editor-json-import-fix).

## Quick answers to common questions

**Q: The follower count does not match the description during testing. What now?** Screenshot it as evidence first. Do not change the password. Submit it as a delivery mismatch for verification.

**Q: I already changed the password. Can I still claim?** Changing the password or rebinding contact details alters account state and normally removes eligibility under the first-login guarantee. The exact outcome depends on the account state.

**Q: The account logs in but gets zero views. Is the account bad?** It may be rate limiting, a fingerprint mismatch, or an expired cookie. Align user agent, timezone and language first, then judge.

**Q: Can I rotate several accounts through one device?** Not advisable. Bulk logins from a single device readily trigger risk controls; manage them in isolated environments instead.

Chained together, the three stages form one pipeline: **define the use case → clear the four indicators → run the ten-minute test and archive evidence → submit claims within the stated boundaries → control pace for the first seven days**. Every step lands on a concrete action, which is far cheaper than fixing things afterwards.
